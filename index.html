Who We Are

About PHINEHAS GENERATION FOUNDATION

Founded in 2016 by Tosin AKANO, PHINEHAS GENERATION FOUNDATION is a non-governmental organisation committed to building stronger communities by empowering teenagers and caring for elderly people. What began as a vision to mentor young people has grown into a movement of hope that now reaches families and communities across Nigeria and the United Kingdom.

Our work is built on a simple but profound conviction: that honouring the elderly is not charity — it is a moral obligation. And that guiding a young person today shapes the world for decades to come. Through education, advocacy, humanitarian outreach, and compassionate service, we are building a bridge between two generations that shape every society.

In Nigeria, we walk with the next generation. In the UK, we walk with the elderly. Across both, we bridge two worlds — because when generations connect, communities heal.

Why We Exist
2.1 billion

By 2050, 2.1 billion people will be over 60 — and 80% of them will live in countries least equipped to care for them.

That's not a distant statistic. That's a mother, a father, a grandparent — someone's whole world. It's the elderly woman down the street who hasn't seen a doctor in years. It's the widower who eats alone every day. It's the generation that built the world we now live in, quietly fading into the background.

At the same time, the world is experiencing the largest youth population in human history. Over 3 billion people are under 25 — and most live in regions where opportunity is scarce. Without guidance, mentorship, and hope, their potential remains untapped.

PHINEHAS GENERATION FOUNDATION exists at the intersection of these two realities. We are not waiting for governments or institutions to solve these problems. We are building bridges — one teenager, one elder, one community at a time.
