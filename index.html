Who We Are

About PHINEHAS GENERATION

Founded in 2016 by Tosin AKANO, PHINEHAS GENERATION FOUNDATION is a non-governmental organisation committed to building stronger communities by empowering teenagers and caring for elderly people.

What began as a vision to mentor young people has grown into a movement of hope that now reaches families and communities across Nigeria and the United Kingdom — through education, advocacy, humanitarian outreach, and compassionate service.

In Nigeria, we walk with the next generation. In the UK, we walk with the elderly. Across both, we bridge two worlds — because when generations connect, communities heal.

I thank God and PHINEHAS GENERATION FOUNDATION for restoring my hope.

Mrs. Lawal Abeke was living with severe cataracts that took her independence. Through our medical outreach, she received a full eye examination, cataract surgery, and prescription glasses. She can see her grandchildren again.

Her story is one of hundreds. Every pair of glasses, every surgery, every food pack — they are not transactions. They are lives restored, dignity returned, hope rekindled.

Why We Exist
2.1 billion

By 2050, 2.1 billion people will be over 60 — and 80% of them will live in countries least equipped to care for them.

That's not a distant statistic. That's a mother, a father, a grandparent — someone's whole world. At the same time, the world is experiencing the largest youth population in human history. Over 3 billion people are under 25.

PHINEHAS GENERATION FOUNDATION exists at the intersection of these two realities. We are not waiting for governments or institutions. We are building bridges — one teenager, one elder, one community at a time.

Our Presence

Where We Work

Two countries. Two generations. One mission.

Nigeria

Youth & Teenagers

Our strongest presence. Nigeria is where PHINEHAS GENERATION began, and it remains our primary focus for youth development. Through school outreach, mentorship programs, and leadership training, we are raising the next generation of ethical, capable Nigerian leaders.

We also serve elderly communities through medical outreach and welfare programs — because honouring the elderly is at the heart of everything we do.

United Kingdom

Elderly People

In the UK, our focus is the elderly. Many older people in the UK face isolation, loneliness, and barriers to care. Through companionship programs, welfare support, and community outreach, we bring dignity and connection to those who have given so much to society.
