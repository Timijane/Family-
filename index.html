Who We Are

About PHINEHAS GENERATION FOUNDATION

Founded in 2016 by Mrs. Oluwatosin Balogun Akano, PHINEHAS GENERATION FOUNDATION is a non-governmental organization committed to building stronger communities by empowering teenagers and caring for elderly people.

What started as a vision to mentor young people has grown into a movement of hope that now reaches families, communities, and senior citizens through education, advocacy, humanitarian outreach, and compassionate service.

From our beginnings in Abeokuta to our expansion into Lagos and the United Kingdom, our commitment remains the same: raising tomorrow's leaders while honouring yesterday's heroes.

Our Leader

Meet Our Founder

A Vision Born from Compassion

Founder

Mrs. Oluwatosin Balogun Akano

Founder & Executive Director

Behind every life transformed by PHINEHAS GENERATION FOUNDATION is a woman whose heart has always been drawn to people. In 2016, Mrs. Oluwatosin Balogun Akano founded the organization with a simple but profound conviction: that every young person deserves guidance, and every elderly person deserves to be valued.

What began as a passion to mentor teenagers has grown into a movement that empowers young people, restores dignity to senior citizens, and inspires communities to care for one another. Her unwavering commitment to compassion, service, and human development continues to shape every program, outreach, and life touched by the foundation.

"Raising tomorrow's leaders while honouring yesterday's heroes."
