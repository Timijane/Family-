Featured Story

Restoring Hope Through Sight

"I thank God and PHINEHAS GENERATION FOUNDATION for restoring my hope."

Mrs. Lawal Abeke had been living with severe cataracts that significantly impaired her vision. She could no longer see the faces of her grandchildren. She had lost her independence.

Through our medical outreach, she received a comprehensive eye examination, cataract surgery, and prescription glasses — all free of charge. Today, she can see again.

She is one of hundreds. Every pair of glasses, every surgery, every medication administered is a life restored. Not just her sight — her dignity, her confidence, her hope.

Igbore, Abeokuta, Ogun State

Our Presence

Communities We Serve

Two countries. Multiple communities. One mission.

Nigeria

Youth & Elderly

Our founding home. Through schools, churches, and community centres across Ogun State, we run mentorship programs for teenagers and outreach initiatives for elderly people.

Our medical outreaches and community welfare programs have reached hundreds of vulnerable seniors.

Abiola Way, Abeokuta Igbore, Abeokuta Obosokoto Egba Comprehensive High School OOPL, Abeokuta

United Kingdom

Elderly People

In the UK, our founder works directly with social workers to reach isolated elderly people — providing companionship, welfare support, and connection to vital services.

Our approach is partnership-based: we work alongside established welfare systems to fill the gaps where no one else is reaching.

Registration in Progress

PHINEHAS GENERATION FOUNDATION is currently in the process of obtaining formal registration with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) of Nigeria. This registration will enable us to formalise our operations, access institutional funding, and expand our reach further.

In the meantime, we remain committed to full transparency. All our work — every medical outreach, every mentorship program, every community initiative — is conducted with the highest standards of accountability and integrity.
