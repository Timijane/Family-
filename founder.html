Meet Our Founder

The heart, vision, and leadership behind PHINEHAS GENERATION FOUNDATION

Home / About Us / Founder
Founder & Executive Director
By PHINEHAS GENERATION FOUNDATION Published 2026

In the heart of Abeokuta, Ogun State, a quiet revolution has been unfolding since 2016—one led not by politicians or celebrities, but by a woman whose compassion for people has become the driving force behind a movement that is transforming lives across generations. Mrs. Oluwatosin Balogun Akano, founder and executive director of PHINEHAS GENERATION FOUNDATION, has dedicated her life to bridging the gap between the young and the elderly, ensuring that no one is left behind.

Her journey began with a simple observation: many teenagers were struggling silently. Low self-esteem, peer pressure, depression, abuse, identity crises, and uncertainty about the future had become common challenges among young people. While many recognized these problems, few were willing to step forward and offer solutions. Mrs. Akano chose to be among the few who acted.

A Vision Born from Compassion

In 2016, Mrs. Akano founded PHINEHAS GENERATION FOUNDATION with a clear mission: to provide mentorship, guidance, and leadership development for teenagers. What began as small gatherings in Abeokuta quickly grew into a vibrant movement. Through conferences, seminars, leadership workshops, school outreaches, and community engagements, the foundation created safe spaces where young people could openly discuss issues affecting their lives and receive practical guidance from experienced professionals.

Topics such as self-esteem, sexual health, sexual purity, mental wellbeing, leadership, purpose discovery, and character development became central to the foundation's work. The approach was holistic, addressing not just academic or career concerns but the whole person.

"One person's compassion has the power to transform countless lives. I have seen it happen, and I believe it can happen for every person who is willing to care." — Mrs. Oluwatosin Balogun Akano
A Landmark Moment

One of the organization's defining moments came in 2018, when PHINEHAS GENERATION FOUNDATION organized a youth hangout at the Obasanjo Presidential Library in Abeokuta. The event brought together teenagers from different backgrounds for a day of learning, inspiration, and personal development. Through mentorship, interactive sessions, and inspirational talks, many participants discovered new confidence, clarified their goals, and found renewed purpose.

Today, many of those young people have completed higher education and are contributing meaningfully to their communities as graduates and professionals. Their success stories stand as a lasting testament to the power of mentorship and timely intervention.

Expanding the Vision

As the foundation's work continued to grow, another need began to weigh heavily on Mrs. Akano's heart. While visiting communities, she encountered many elderly men and women living with loneliness, declining health, and limited access to basic care. Some struggled with poor eyesight that affected their daily lives, while others simply longed for companionship and the reassurance that they had not been forgotten.

These experiences transformed the direction of the foundation. Mrs. Akano believed that a society cannot truly thrive if it invests only in its future while neglecting those who helped build its past. This conviction led to the expansion of PHINEHAS GENERATION FOUNDATION's mission to include comprehensive care for senior citizens.

🔍 KEY INITIATIVE

Medical Outreach Program

Providing free healthcare services, eye examinations, cataract surgeries, and prescription glasses to elderly people in underserved communities.

❤️ IMPACT

Community Welfare Support

Food distribution, financial assistance, regular welfare visits, and emotional care for senior citizens across Nigeria.

"I believe that every life has value and every person deserves the opportunity to live with dignity, hope, and purpose." — Mrs. Oluwatosin Balogun Akano
Growing Beyond Borders

From its beginnings in Abeokuta, PHINEHAS GENERATION FOUNDATION expanded its activities into Lagos and has since extended its reach to the United Kingdom. Under Mrs. Akano's leadership, the organization continues to build partnerships and explore new opportunities to increase its impact while remaining faithful to its founding mission.

Today, the foundation serves as a bridge between generations—preparing young people for the future while honoring and caring for the elderly. Mrs. Akano believes that lasting change begins with compassion. She believes that every teenager deserves guidance, every elderly person deserves dignity, and every community becomes stronger when people choose to care for one another.

Looking Ahead

Mrs. Akano envisions a future where PHINEHAS GENERATION FOUNDATION reaches even more communities across Nigeria, the United Kingdom, and beyond. Her goal is to expand youth empowerment initiatives, strengthen healthcare and welfare programs for the elderly, establish sustainable community development projects, and build strategic partnerships that multiply the foundation's impact for generations to come.

Although much has been accomplished since 2016, she believes the journey has only just begun. With faith, compassion, and unwavering commitment, she continues to lead a movement dedicated to empowering the young, honoring the elderly, and transforming communities—one life at a time.

Together, we can build communities where compassion connects generations and no one is left behind."
