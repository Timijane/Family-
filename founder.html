Founder & Executive Director
Founder & Executive Director

Mrs. Oluwatosin Balogun Akano

"

One person's compassion has the power to transform countless lives.

Behind every vision that changes lives is someone who chooses to see possibility where others see problems. For Mrs. Oluwatosin Balogun Akano, that choice became the beginning of a journey that has impacted young people, restored hope to elderly citizens, and inspired communities to care more deeply for one another.

As the Founder and Executive Director of PHINEHAS GENERATION FOUNDATION, Mrs. Akano has devoted herself to building a future where compassion, purpose, and service are not just ideals but everyday realities. Her leadership is rooted in a genuine love for people and an unwavering belief that every individual—regardless of age, background, or circumstance—deserves to be seen, valued, and empowered.

A Vision Born from Compassion

In 2016, while observing the challenges facing many teenagers, Mrs. Akano recognized a growing need for intentional mentorship and guidance. She encountered young people struggling with low self-esteem, peer pressure, depression, abuse, identity crises, and uncertainty about their future. Many possessed incredible potential but lacked the encouragement and support needed to realize it.

Rather than simply acknowledging these challenges, she chose to respond with action.

Guided by a desire to see lives transformed, she founded PHINEHAS GENERATION FOUNDATION as a platform where teenagers could receive mentorship, develop leadership skills, build confidence, and discover purpose. Her vision was never limited to organizing events; it was to shape lives and empower a generation to become responsible leaders, compassionate citizens, and agents of positive change.

Building a Generation of Purpose

What began as a small initiative soon grew into a vibrant movement. Through conferences, mentorship sessions, leadership workshops, school outreaches, and community engagements, Mrs. Akano created safe spaces where young people could openly discuss issues affecting their lives and receive practical guidance from experienced professionals.

Topics such as self-esteem, sexual health, sexual purity, mental wellbeing, leadership, purpose discovery, and character development became central to the foundation's work.

"One of the organization's defining moments came in 2018, when PHINEHAS GENERATION FOUNDATION organized a youth hangout at the Obasanjo Presidential Library, Abeokuta. The event brought together teenagers from different backgrounds for a day of learning, inspiration, and personal development."

— PHINEHAS GENERATION FOUNDATION Archive

Many of those participants have since completed their education and are now graduates contributing meaningfully to their communities—a lasting testament to the power of mentorship and timely intervention.

Expanding the Vision

As the foundation's work continued to grow, another need began to weigh heavily on Mrs. Akano's heart.

While visiting communities, she encountered many elderly men and women living with loneliness, declining health, and limited access to basic care. Some struggled with poor eyesight that affected their daily lives, while others simply longed for companionship and the reassurance that they had not been forgotten.

These experiences transformed the direction of the foundation.

Mrs. Akano believed that a society cannot truly thrive if it invests only in its future while neglecting those who helped build its past. This conviction led to the expansion of PHINEHAS GENERATION FOUNDATION's mission to include comprehensive care for senior citizens.

Today, the foundation provides medical outreaches, eye care services, food distribution, welfare support, financial assistance, and emotional care for elderly people across communities.

"One particularly memorable outreach took place in Igbore, Abeokuta, where an elderly beneficiary, Mrs. Lawal Abeke, regained her sight after receiving cataract treatment and prescription glasses through the foundation's medical outreach program. Witnessing the joy and renewed hope on her face reaffirmed Mrs. Akano's belief that even a single act of kindness can transform a life."

— PHINEHAS GENERATION FOUNDATION Impact Report

A Leader Who Serves

Mrs. Akano's leadership is defined not only by the programs she has established but by the values she demonstrates every day. She believes that true leadership is expressed through service, humility, integrity, and compassion.

Her approach has always been people-centered. Whether mentoring a teenager, encouraging a volunteer, comforting an elderly beneficiary, or building partnerships with organizations, she leads with empathy and a genuine commitment to making a lasting difference.

For her, success is measured not by titles or recognition but by the lives transformed, the hope restored, and the opportunities created for others.

Growing Beyond Borders

From its beginnings in Abeokuta, Ogun State, PHINEHAS GENERATION FOUNDATION expanded its activities into Lagos and has since extended its reach to the United Kingdom. Under Mrs. Akano's leadership, the organization continues to build partnerships and explore new opportunities to increase its impact while remaining faithful to its founding mission.

Today, the foundation serves as a bridge between generations—preparing young people for the future while honoring and caring for the elderly.

Her Philosophy

Mrs. Akano believes that lasting change begins with compassion.

She believes that every teenager deserves guidance, every elderly person deserves dignity, and every community becomes stronger when people choose to care for one another.

This philosophy continues to guide every initiative, outreach program, partnership, and decision made by PHINEHAS GENERATION FOUNDATION.

Looking Ahead

Mrs. Akano envisions a future where PHINEHAS GENERATION FOUNDATION reaches even more communities across Nigeria, the United Kingdom, and beyond. Her goal is to expand youth empowerment initiatives, strengthen healthcare and welfare programs for the elderly, establish sustainable community development projects, and build strategic partnerships that multiply the foundation's impact for generations to come.

Although much has been accomplished since 2016, she believes the journey has only just begun.

With faith, compassion, and unwavering commitment, she continues to lead a movement dedicated to empowering the young, honoring the elderly, and transforming communities—one life at a time.

Through PHINEHAS GENERATION FOUNDATION, we are committed to raising confident young leaders while restoring hope to elderly people who deserve our love, care, and respect. Together, we can build communities where compassion connects generations and no one is left behind."
