Our Story

A Vision That Began with One Heart

And Grew into a Movement of Hope

Every meaningful journey begins with a single step. For PHINEHAS GENERATION FOUNDATION, that step was taken in 2016 by Mrs. Oluwatosin Balogun Akano, whose compassion for people became the seed of a vision that continues to transform lives today.

At a time when many teenagers were silently struggling with low self-esteem, depression, peer pressure, abuse, and uncertainty about their future, Mrs. Akano recognized a pressing need for guidance and mentorship. She believed that with the right support, every young person could discover purpose, make wise decisions, and become a positive influence in society.

Driven by this conviction, she established PHINEHAS GENERATION FOUNDATION as a platform where teenagers could learn, grow, ask questions freely, and receive practical guidance on issues that shape their lives. What began as small gatherings and mentoring sessions quickly grew into conferences, seminars, leadership workshops, and community outreaches that inspired hundreds of young people.

A Landmark Moment

One of the foundation's landmark moments came in 2018, when teenagers from different communities gathered at the Obasanjo Presidential Library in Abeokuta for a life-changing youth hangout. Through mentorship, interactive sessions, and inspirational talks, many participants discovered new confidence, clarified their goals, and found renewed purpose. Today, many of those young people have completed higher education and are contributing meaningfully to society as graduates and professionals.

"What started as small gatherings and mentoring sessions quickly grew into conferences, seminars, leadership workshops, and community outreaches that inspired hundreds of young people."

Expanding the Vision

As the foundation expanded its activities across Abeokuta and later into Magodo, Lagos, another need became increasingly evident. During community visits and outreach programs, the team encountered many elderly men and women who had been left behind by society. Some struggled with poor health, failing eyesight, loneliness, and financial hardship. Others simply longed for someone to listen, care, and remind them that they still mattered.

These encounters transformed the foundation's mission. Recognizing that a truly compassionate society must care for both its future and its past, PHINEHAS GENERATION FOUNDATION broadened its focus to include elderly care. Medical outreaches, free eye examinations, cataract interventions, prescription glasses, food distribution, financial support, and regular welfare visits became integral to the organization's work.

A Story of Restored Hope

One particularly memorable outreach took place in Igbore, Abeokuta, where Mrs. Lawal Abeke, an elderly woman living with severe cataracts, received life-changing medical attention. Following successful treatment and the provision of prescription glasses, she regained her sight and expressed overwhelming joy that the hope she thought she had lost had finally been restored. Her story remains a powerful reminder of why the foundation exists—to restore hope where it has faded and to remind every person that they are valued.

Looking Ahead

Over the years, PHINEHAS GENERATION FOUNDATION has grown beyond its humble beginnings. What started as a local initiative in Ogun State has expanded into Lagos and now reaches communities in the United Kingdom, building partnerships and extending its impact across borders while remaining rooted in its original mission of service.

Today, PHINEHAS GENERATION FOUNDATION stands as a bridge between generations. We empower teenagers to become confident, responsible, and purpose-driven leaders while ensuring that elderly people receive the dignity, care, and respect they deserve.

Although our reach continues to grow, our purpose remains unchanged. Every program we organize, every community we visit, every volunteer we mobilize, and every life we touch reflects our unwavering commitment to building a society where no young person is left without guidance and no elderly person is forgotten.

Our story is still being written. With every life transformed, every partnership formed, and every act of kindness shared, we move one step closer to realizing our vision of a world where compassion unites generations, hope overcomes hardship, and every individual has the opportunity to thrive.

"This is our history. This is our purpose. This is the legacy we are building—one life, one family, and one generation at a time."
