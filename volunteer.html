Volunteer Requirements

What you need to know before joining us.

General Requirements

  • Must be at least 18 years old
  • Passion for serving young people and the elderly
  • Willingness to commit to regular volunteer hours
  • Ability to work well in a team environment
  • Respect for all individuals regardless of background
  • Good communication and interpersonal skills

What We Provide

  • Comprehensive volunteer training
  • Ongoing support and supervision
  • Certificate of volunteer service
  • Opportunity to make a real difference
  • A welcoming and supportive community
  • References for future opportunities
